# DaemonBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DaemonBot (the "Bot") and its web control panel (the "Panel"), together the "Service", collect, how that information is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you. The Service is operated by **[OPERATOR NAME]** ("we", "us").

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Members:** anyone in a Discord server ("Server") where the Bot has been added, whether or not they use its commands.
- **Panel users:** anyone who signs in to the Panel with Discord.

The Service works within Discord, which handles your account under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). This policy covers only what the Service itself does.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Moderation and server records

- **Server events:** members joining and leaving; nickname, username and display-name changes; role and timeout changes; bans, unbans and kicks; changes to channels, roles, emoji, stickers, webhooks, invites and server settings; and actions taken by Discord's AutoMod. Each record includes the member's Discord user ID, username and avatar.
- **Message content:** the Bot reads messages as they are sent so it can apply the word filter. Messages are not stored when they are sent. They are recorded only when they are deleted or edited: the text of a deleted message, and the before-and-after text of an edited message, go into the Server's log.
- **Invites:** which invite a member joined with, who created it, and how many times it has been used.
- **Moderation records:** warnings, together with their reasons and the moderator who gave them.
- **Tickets:** the subject and details of support tickets, who opened and claimed each one, and its status. The ticket conversation itself stays in Discord.

### 2.2 Activity statistics

Unless a Server's administrators turn them off, the Service keeps the following statistics for each member:

- **Messages:** how many messages and reactions a member posts, the channels they post in, the first and last days they were active, and a count for each of the past 30 days. Only counts are kept, not message text.
- **Voice:** how much time a member spends in each voice channel, how many sessions they have had, and which other members were in the channel with them and for how long.
- **Games:** the games Discord shows a member playing (their "Rich Presence"), and how long and how often they play each one. This record covers every Server the Bot shares with the member.
- **Interactions:** how often members reply to, mention and react to each other, talk in the same channel, and spend time together in voice or in the same game. These are combined into a score for each pair of members. The scores are used to show which members interact most and which groups of members tend to be active together.
- **Server totals:** daily counts such as messages per channel, active members, joins and leaves, commands used, and log events by type.

**Opting out:** any member can opt out of these statistics, one Server at a time, by emailing smiledaemon77@gmail.com. The email should name each Server to opt out of (by name or ID) and include the member's Discord username or user ID. Once an opt-out is applied for a Server:

- The Service stops keeping the member's message, voice and interaction statistics in that Server, and deletes the ones already kept there, including the member's entries in other members' voice and interaction records in that Server.
- Games seen through that Server are no longer counted, and the member's games are no longer shown to that Server's administrators. Because the game record covers every Server the Bot shares with the member, it is deleted only once the member has opted out of all of those Servers, or on request (Section 7).
- Other Servers are not affected. To opt out of more than one Server, name each of them.

Server totals do not identify individual members and are not affected. Moderation and server records (Section 2.1) are also not affected.

### 2.3 Panel users

When you sign in to the Panel, Discord gives us your user ID, username, avatar and the list of Servers you belong to. We use that list only to work out which Servers you may manage. A sign-in token is stored in your browser. We store only a cryptographic hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the token, never the token itself. We also record every change you make through the Panel, along with your name.

### 2.4 What we do not collect

We do not collect your email address, password, payment details, IP address (beyond what our hosting providers log automatically, see Section 5), direct messages, or messages in channels the Bot cannot see.

## 3. How we use information

- **Moderation and server management:** filtering messages, logging events for administrators, handling tickets and keeping warning records.
- **Community insights:** helping a Server's administrators understand how their Server is used. They can view activity charts, rankings, individual members' activity, and the interaction statistics described in Section 2.2.
- **Operating the Service:** fixing problems, preventing abuse and understanding which features are used.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

## 4. Who can see it

- **Server administrators:** members with Manage Server or Administrator can see the information in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 for their own Server, through the Panel and the Server's log channel. This includes each member's activity and interaction statistics. For games and voice, they see only activity linked to their own Server.
- **The operator:** we can see the information for every Server the Bot is in.
- **Moderators and staff:** anyone who can read a Server's log channel can see the entries posted there.

## 6. How long we keep it

| Information | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Log entries in the Panel's database, including deleted and edited message text | 90 days |
| Daily Server statistics | 400 days |
| Per-day message counts for each member | 30 days |
| Per-member activity, voice, game and interaction totals | Until deleted on request, or until the Server's administrators delete them |
| Warnings and tickets | Until deleted by the Server's administrators or on request |
| Entries posted in a Server's log channel | Until that Server's moderators delete them (they are ordinary Discord messages) |
| Panel sign-ins | 7 days |
| Record of changes made through the Panel | 30 days |
| Each Server's latest snapshot | Replaced on every update, and deleted when the Bot leaves the Server |

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Server administrators** can turn game, voice, activity and interaction statistics off in the Panel's Settings. Collection of that information then stops for the Server.
- **Members** can opt out of activity statistics in a Server by emailing smiledaemon77@gmail.com and naming that Server, as described in Section 2.2. Each Server needs its own request. They can also hide game activity by turning off Discord's *Share your activity status* setting. They can stop all collection in a Server by leaving it. Leaving does not delete information already collected. To have it deleted, contact us.
- **Access, correction and deletion:** you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us (Section 11). We will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm that you control the Discord account in question.
- **Objection:** depending on where you live, you may have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Section 8), or to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

We process this information on the basis of legitimate interests: ours, and those of each Server's administrators, in moderating, securing, running and understanding their communities. For Panel users, we process sign-in information to provide the Panel you asked to use.

## 9. Children

Discord requires its users to be at least 13 years old, or older where local law requires. The Service is not directed at children under that age, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe we hold such information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security and changes

Data is sent only over encrypted connections. The Panel is open only to signed-in administrators of each Server, and to the operator. No system is perfectly secure.

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date and announce them in the Bot's support server or through the Panel before they take effect.
