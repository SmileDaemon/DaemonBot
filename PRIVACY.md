# DaemonBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DaemonBot (the "Bot") and its web control panel (the "Panel"), together the "Service", collect, how that information is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you. The Service is operated by **SmileDaemon** ("we", "us").

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Members:** anyone in a Discord server ("Server") where the Bot has been added, whether or not they use its commands.
- **Panel users:** anyone who signs in to the Panel with Discord.

The Service works within Discord, which handles your account under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). This policy covers only what the Service itself does.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Moderation and server records

- **Server events:** changes such as members joining or leaving, role and nickname updates, moderation actions, and updates to channels, roles, invites and server settings. Records include the member's Discord user ID, username and avatar.
- **Messages:** the Bot reads messages to apply the word filter but does not store them. If a message is edited or deleted, its text is recorded in the Server's log.
- **Invites:** which invite a member used to join, and who created it.
- **Warnings and tickets:** warnings and their reasons, and the details and status of support tickets.

### 2.2 Usage statistics

Unless a Server's administrators turn them off, the Service keeps usage statistics for the Server's members:

- **Messages:** message and reaction counts, and the channels they were posted in. Message text is not kept.
- **Voice:** time spent in voice channels.
- **Games:** games shown in a member's Discord activity status, and time spent in them.
- **Interactions:** counts of replies, mentions and reactions between members, and time spent together in voice or games.
- **Server totals:** daily totals such as messages, active members, joins and leaves.

**Opting out:** members can opt out of these statistics in a Server by emailing smiledaemon77@gmail.com with the Server's name or ID and their Discord username or user ID. Each Server needs its own request. Statistics already kept about the member in that Server are deleted, and no more are kept there. Game statistics are shared across Servers, so they are deleted once the member has opted out of every Server they share with the Bot. Server totals are not affected.

### 2.3 Panel users

When you sign in to the Panel, Discord shares your user ID, username, avatar and Server list, which we use only to determine which Servers you can manage. We also keep a secure sign-in record and a record of changes you make through the Panel.

### 2.4 What we do not collect

We do not collect your email address, password, payment details, IP address (beyond what our hosting providers log automatically, see Section 5), direct messages, or messages in channels the Bot cannot see.

## 3. How we use information

- **Moderation and server management:** filtering messages, logging events for administrators, handling tickets and keeping warning records.
- **Community insights:** helping a Server's administrators understand how their Server is used. They can view the usage statistics described in Section 2.2.
- **Operating the Service:** fixing problems, preventing abuse and understanding which features are used.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

## 4. Who can see it

- **Server administrators:** members with Manage Server or Administrator can see the information in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 for their own Server, through the Panel and the Server's log channel. For games and voice, they see only time spent in their own Server.
- **The operator:** we can see the information for every Server the Bot is in.
- **Moderators and staff:** anyone who can read a Server's log channel can see the entries posted there.

## 5. Service providers

| Provider | Role | What it handles |
|---|---|---|

## 6. How long we keep it

| Information | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Log entries in the Panel's database, including deleted and edited message text | 90 days |
| Daily Server statistics | 400 days |
| Daily message counts | 30 days |
| Usage statistics | Until deleted on request, or until the Server's administrators delete them |
| Warnings and tickets | Until deleted by the Server's administrators or on request |
| Entries posted in a Server's log channel | Until that Server's moderators delete them (they are ordinary Discord messages) |
| Panel sign-ins | 7 days |
| Record of changes made through the Panel | 30 days |
| Each Server's latest snapshot | Replaced on every update, and deleted when the Bot leaves the Server |

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Server administrators** can turn usage statistics off in the Panel's Settings. Collection of that information then stops for the Server.
- **Members** can opt out of usage statistics in a Server by emailing smiledaemon77@gmail.com and naming that Server, as described in Section 2.2. Each Server needs its own request. They can also hide game activity by turning off Discord's *Share your activity status* setting. They can stop all collection in a Server by leaving it. Leaving does not delete information already collected. To have it deleted, contact us.
- **Access, correction and deletion:** you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us (Section 11). We will respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm that you control the Discord account in question.
- **Objection:** depending on where you live, you may have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Section 8), or to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

We process this information on the basis of legitimate interests: ours, and those of each Server's administrators, in moderating, securing, running and understanding their communities. For Panel users, we process sign-in information to provide the Panel you asked to use.

## 9. Children

Discord requires its users to be at least 13 years old, or older where local law requires. The Service is not directed at children under that age, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe we hold such information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security and changes

Data is sent only over encrypted connections. The Panel is open only to signed-in administrators of each Server, and to the operator. No system is perfectly secure.

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date and announce them in the Bot's support server or through the Panel before they take effect.
