# How matching works:
#   - Whole words only: "coon" catches "coon" but not "raccoon".
#   - Capitals, accents, l33t-speak (n1gg3r), stretched letters (fagggot) and
#     dots/dashes between letters (f.a.g) are all caught automatically.
#   - Plurals are caught automatically (word -> words).
#   - End an entry with * to catch anything starting with it: badword* catches badwording.
#     Careful: this can catch innocent longer words too.
#   - Phrases work: put the words on one line separated by spaces.
#
